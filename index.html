Is Starhela legit? Is it a real earning platform?
Yes — Starhela is a registered digital earning platform that has paid out to thousands of users across Africa including Kenya, Uganda, Nigeria, and Tanzania. Payments are processed through official channels including M-Pesa, Airtel Money, MTN, and bank transfer.
How does chatting with foreigners work on Starhela?
Once your account is activated, log in and open the Chat section. You'll be matched with people from countries like UK, USA, Canada, Qatar who want to talk. You chat via text and earn money for every hour spent chatting, per message, per session, and through tips. No video calls — just text chat from your phone.
How much does Starhela registration cost in Kenya?
Starhela registration is free. There is a one-time account activation fee of $5 (KSH 550). This unlocks your full account — all 12+ earning activities including chat with foreigners, instant withdrawals. You pay once and the account is yours for life. No monthly fees or hidden charges.
How do I withdraw my Starhela earnings via M-Pesa?
Earnings are credited to your Starhela wallet as you complete tasks. You can request a withdrawal at any time. Withdrawals are processed within minutes to hours via M-Pesa, Airtel Money, MTN Mobile Money, and bank transfer.
Do I need special skills to chat with foreigners?
No special skills required. You just need basic English, a smartphone, and an internet connection. The foreigners you chat with are looking for friendly conversation — just be yourself and you'll earn. Starhela provides guidelines to help you chat effectively and maximize your earnings.
How much can I earn per month on Starhela?
Earnings depend on your activity level. Active Starhela members in Kenya earn between KSH 50,000 and KSH 105,000 per month. The chat task is the highest paying, with top earners making up to $20/day. The more time you spend chatting, the more you earn.
